Private Heli-Charter

Exceptional Value

Empty Legs

When a private jet is repositioning after dropping off its previous passengers, it flies as an "empty leg" — and NovansJets offers these flights to our clients at dramatically reduced rates. This presents an extraordinary opportunity to experience ultra-long-range jets and supermidsize aircraft at a fraction of the normal charter cost.

Our real-time empty leg database is updated continuously. Register with us to receive automatic notifications matching your preferred routes, aircraft categories, and travel dates.

Savings of 25–75% vs. standard charter rates
Real-time route-matching notifications
Identical service standards to full-price charters
Available on light jets through to ultra-long-range aircraft

Critical Missions

Medevac & Cargo

When time is critical, NovansJets' dedicated medevac and cargo division provides a rapid, professional response. Our air ambulance fleet operates 24/7 with certified medical crew, full ICU equipment suites, and a global network of approved medical facilities at destination.

For time-sensitive freight — from critical automotive parts to pharmaceutical consignments and valuables — our cargo division provides door-to-door solutions with full chain-of-custody documentation and white-glove handling.

Exclusive Access

Jet Membership & Fractional Ownership

For those who travel privately more than 50 hours per year, NovansJets' membership and fractional ownership programmes offer unbeatable economics without the burden of full aircraft ownership. Our tiered membership tiers — Signature, Prestige, and Elite — provide guaranteed aircraft availability, fixed hourly rates, and a curated concierge experience from day one.

Fractional share ownership starts at 1/16th of a specific aircraft type, with full cost transparency, guaranteed residual value schedules, and seamless exit provisions managed by our aviation finance team.
