About NovansJets

Fifteen years of curating the world's finest private aviation experiences — driven by an uncompromising commitment to safety, discretion, and excellence.

Our Philosophy

More Than a Flight.
A Standard of Living.

NovansJets was founded on a single conviction: that private aviation, when done correctly, is not a luxury — it is a necessity for those whose time is irreplaceable. We exist to remove the friction, the compromise, and the unpredictability from air travel, delivering instead an experience defined by certainty, elegance, and care.

We are not a marketplace or a broker aggregator. We are a curated service — a team of aviation professionals who build every itinerary by hand, who know our clients by name, and who treat every flight as if our own reputation depends on it. Because it does.

From our origins managing a single Gulfstream G450 in 2009 to today's global network spanning over 180 destinations on six continents, NovansJets has grown through referral and repeat business. We have never advertised our services publicly. Every client comes to us by recommendation.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Uncompromising Safety

Every operator in our network holds ARG/US Gold or Platinum certification. We conduct independent safety audits, verify crew type-ratings, and review maintenance logs before confirming any booking. Safety is not a department — it is our culture.

Absolute Discretion

Client privacy is sacrosanct at NovansJets. We operate under strict NDAs with all crew and ground staff. Passenger manifests, itineraries, and travel patterns are never shared, never sold, and never referenced without explicit consent.

Curated Excellence

We curate — not aggregate. Every aircraft, caterer, and ground handler in our network has been personally vetted by our team. We accept only what meets our standard, regardless of price pressure or availability.

Personal Relationships

Every NovansJets client has a named account manager who knows their preferences — seat configuration, preferred catering, music playlists, dietary requirements. We remember so our clients never have to ask twice.

Our Commitment

Safety Standards

At NovansJets, safety is the foundation upon which every service is built. We do not simply verify that an operator holds a licence — we conduct a deep due diligence process before adding any partner to our network.

IS-BAO

International Standard for Business Aircraft Operations certification required for all operators in our managed fleet.

ARG/US

Minimum ARG/US Gold rating across all charter operators in our network, with preference for Platinum-rated providers.

WYVERN

Wyvern Wingman audit compliance checked on all operators. Crew records, recency of type, and rest hours are verified for every flight.

IATA

IATA Operational Safety Audit (IOSA) registration required for all NovansJets managed aircraft operations exceeding 200 hours per year.
