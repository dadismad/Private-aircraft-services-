Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2024. Your privacy and data security are fundamental to NovansJets.

This Privacy Policy describes how NovansJets Ltd ("NovansJets", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use our website at novansjets.com and related services. By using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Who We Are

NovansJets Ltd is a private aviation services company registered in the State of New York, USA, and operating a UK subsidiary registered in England and Wales (Companies House No. 12345678). Our registered address is 100 Park Avenue, Suite 1600, New York, NY 10017, USA.

For data protection purposes in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom, NovansJets Ltd acts as a data controller. Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at privacy@novansjets.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry or request a quote, we collect:

  • Full name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Travel details (departure and arrival locations, travel dates, number of passengers)
  • Service preferences and any special requirements
  • Payment and billing information (processed by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor)
  • Passport and travel document details required to meet regulatory flight manifest obligations

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect:

  • IP address, browser type and version, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, referral source
  • Device identifiers and cookie data (see Section 8)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources including travel agents, corporate travel managers, and referral partners who have your consent to share such data with us.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To process charter bookings, coordinate flights, arrange ground transportation, and provide all contracted services.
  • Communication: To respond to enquiries, send booking confirmations, and provide service updates.
  • Safety & Regulatory Compliance: Flight manifests and passenger details are required by aviation authorities and must be provided to operators, air traffic control, and border agencies as required by law.
  • Account Management: To manage your client account, maintain your preferences, and personalise your experience.
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with applicable laws including anti-money laundering (AML), sanctions screening, and tax reporting obligations.
  • Service Improvement: To analyse usage patterns and improve our website and services (anonymised or aggregated data only).

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent legislation, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract Performance: Processing necessary to fulfil your charter booking and related services.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing required to comply with aviation regulations, customs requirements, and applicable law.
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing necessary for our legitimate business interests (fraud prevention, security, service improvement) where these are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: For marketing communications and non-essential cookies, where you have given explicit consent.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

NovansJets does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Charter Operators: Passenger details necessary for flight operations are shared with the certified operator providing your aircraft, solely for the purpose of fulfilling your booking.
  • Ground Handlers & Service Providers: Name and contact details are shared with ground transportation, catering, and FBO services contracted on your behalf.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Passenger manifests and travel document data are provided to aviation authorities, customs, and immigration services as required by law.
  • Professional Advisors: Our legal, accounting, and insurance advisors under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity subject to equivalent privacy protections.

All third parties who process data on our behalf are bound by data processing agreements requiring them to maintain confidentiality and implement appropriate security measures.

6. International Data Transfers

As a global private aviation service, your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, including the United States and the UAE, where our operations are based. We ensure that such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, or equivalent protections required under applicable data protection law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Specifically:

  • Active client records are retained for the duration of the client relationship plus seven (7) years following the last transaction.
  • Enquiry records where no booking was made are deleted after two (2) years unless you have consented to ongoing marketing communications.
  • Flight manifest data is retained for five (5) years to comply with aviation regulatory requirements.
  • Financial transaction records are retained for seven (7) years to comply with tax and accounting obligations.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. We use:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for website functionality. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how visitors interact with our website (anonymised). Requires your consent.
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your settings and personalise your experience. Requires your consent.

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time via the Cookie Settings link in our footer, or through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data where we no longer have a legal basis for processing.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@novansjets.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

10. Terms of Service (Summary)

By using NovansJets services you agree that: (i) charter services are subject to aircraft availability and operator acceptance; (ii) final pricing is confirmed upon booking confirmation; (iii) cancellation terms vary by aircraft type and timing and are set out in your booking contract; (iv) NovansJets acts as a principal or agent depending on the booking structure, as specified at the time of booking; (v) all disputes are subject to the laws of the State of New York, USA, or England and Wales for UK and EEA clients, as applicable.

Full Terms of Service are provided at the time of booking and are available upon request by contacting charter@novansjets.com.

11. Data Security

NovansJets implements industry-standard technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data. These include TLS encryption for all data in transit, AES-256 encryption for data at rest, multi-factor authentication for all staff systems, regular penetration testing, and strict access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis.

In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the applicable supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected individuals without undue delay.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by email (if you have an account with us) and by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our services following such notification constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

13. Contact

For any questions, requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact:

  • Email: privacy@novansjets.com
  • Post: Data Protection Officer, NovansJets Ltd, 100 Park Avenue, Suite 1600, New York, NY 10017, USA
  • UK Representative: NovansJets UK Ltd, 1 Canada Square, Canary Wharf, London, E14 5AB

Note: This Privacy Policy applies to novansjets.com and all NovansJets client-facing services. It does not apply to third-party websites linked from our website. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use.
