About NovansJets

Curated Private
Aviation, Redefined

NovansJets is a premier private aviation service committed to delivering an unmatched charter experience. We curate every journey with precision — from the moment of enquiry to touchdown — ensuring that every flight reflects the standards our discerning clientele expect.

Our fleet spans ultra-long-range business jets capable of connecting continents nonstop, agile midsize jets for regional missions, and VIP helicopters for seamless city and resort transfers. Every aircraft in our network is IS-BAO or ARG/US Platinum certified.
